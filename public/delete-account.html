Data Deletion Disclosure & Retention Policy:

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and data protection regulations, users may request the deletion of their Pamwill account and associated records. When you submit a deletion request:

  • Data Immediately Erased: Full name, profile avatar, phone number, saved delivery addresses, preferred therapist notes, and authentication credentials.
  • Data Retained: Past completed booking invoices and financial transaction IDs are kept strictly in anonymized format for up to 180 days for taxation and RBI statutory audit compliance, after which they are purged.
